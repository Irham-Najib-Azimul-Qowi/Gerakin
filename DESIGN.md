# Inclusive Motion Design System

## Brand Identity
**Name:** GERAKIN
**Mission:** AI-powered adaptive fitness for wheelchair users.
**Personality:** Bright, Friendly, Inclusive, Cheerful, Modern, Premium.

## Colors

### Primary & Brand
- **Purple (Primary):** `#7C5CFC` — Used for main CTAs, active states, and brand highlights.
- **Sky Blue (Secondary):** `#5EC8FF` — Used for secondary accents, gradients, and supporting visuals.
- **Mint (Accent):** `#7AE7C7` — Used for success states, secondary highlights, and balance.

### Functional
- **Success:** `#4ADE80` — Positive feedback, completed tasks.
- **Warning:** `#FACC15` — Cautionary messages.
- **Error:** `#F87171` — Critical errors or destructive actions.

### Surface & Neutral
- **Background:** `#F8FAFC` — Main app background for a clean, spacious feel.
- **Surface (Card):** `#FFFFFF` — Primary container color.
- **Text Primary:** `#1E293B` — Main headings and body text for high readability.
- **Text Secondary:** `#64748B` — Subheaders, captions, and deactivated states.
- **Border:** `#E2E8F0` — Subtle dividers and container outlines.

## Typography
**Primary Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans
**Base Scale:** 8pt Spacing System

| Style | Weight | Size | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display** | Bold | 32px+ | Hero titles, large stats |
| **Heading** | SemiBold | 20px - 24px | Screen titles, section headers |
| **Body Large** | Medium | 18px | Primary body text, labels |
| **Body Medium**| Regular | 16px | Standard body text, descriptions |
| **Caption** | Regular | 12px - 14px | Metadata, small details |

## Visual Assets

### Illustration Style
- **Type:** Modern flat illustrations.
- **Characteristics:** Rounded shapes, soft gradients (Purple to Sky Blue), inclusive representation (smiling wheelchair users), non-clinical aesthetic.

### Iconography
- **Style:** Rounded outline icons.
- **Inspiration:** Google Material Symbols Rounded.
- **Weight:** Medium stroke (approx. 2px).

## UI Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full-width, Purple background (`#7C5CFC`), White text, 24px border radius.
- **Secondary:** Sky Blue tint or outlined, matching corner radius.
- **Touch Target:** Minimum 48px height for accessibility.

### Cards
- **Style:** White background, 24px border radius, very soft shadow (`0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).
- **Workout Cards:** Feature a thumbnail, title, and metadata chips (duration, level).

### Navigation
- **Bottom Nav:** 5-tab layout (Beranda, Program, Progres, Komunitas, Profil).
- **Active State:** Primary Purple icon with label.
- **Inactive State:** Secondary Text color.

## Accessibility Standards
- **Contrast:** WCAG AA compliance for all text-on-background combinations.
- **Targets:** Large touch areas for limited mobility users.
- **Hierarchy:** Clear progressive disclosure to minimize cognitive load.
- **Feedback:** Visual skeleton overlays for real-time AI movement correction.

## Spacing
- **Grid:** 8pt baseline.
- **Container Padding:** 16px - 24px.
- **Corner Radius:** 24px (Standard Card/Button), 8px (Small Chips).
